Plan-scope note: this evidence was compiled before the later Free-tier revision. Any Cadence one-account/no-AI comparison below is historical; current Free scope is three networks with one account per network (three total), plus limited alt-text/analytics AI. Use the [current requirements](../Cadence-Requirements-and-Decisions.md) and [updated competitive brief](../Cadence-Competitive-Pricing-and-Positioning.md). Checked **5 October 2026** using public official pricing and help pages. USD, excluding taxes; annual equivalents require a yearly commitment. No checkout, account creation or product trial occurred.

## Planable

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual total / effective monthly | Pages per workspace | Users | Created posts per month |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Basic | $39 | $390 / $32.50 | 4 | Unlimited | 60 |
| Pro | $59 | $590 / $49.17 | 10 | Unlimited | 150 |

For five accounts and either one or five users, **Pro is $59 monthly or $590 annually**. Required approvals are included. Adding analytics makes either scenario **$73 monthly or $730 annually**, equivalent to $60.83/month. Inbox is another $9/month or $90/year; listening is $99/month or $990/year. The free allowance is **50 total created posts, with no time limit or reset**. [Official pricing FAQ, updated 4 September 2026](https://help.planable.io/hc/en-us/articles/21715370520092-Questions-on-pricing).

Its strengths include structured collaboration, internal notes/drafts, bulk approvals, version history and AI writing. Pricing is per workspace, with unlimited users. All five Cadence launch networks are listed for scheduling; the reviewed analytics list excludes X. [Official machine-readable pricing guide](https://planable.io/wp-content/uploads/downloads/planable-pricing.md), [live pricing](https://planable.io/pricing/).

Source differences: live cards round Pro's annual equivalent to $49; the FAQ gives the exact $590 annual invoice. Enterprise page limits differ between official documents, so no Enterprise allowance is relied on here. The machine-readable guide says last verified June 2026 and omits the newer listening add-on; the September FAQ controls current prices. Post limits count created records, so they should not be presented as an equivalent number of successful publications across five networks.

## Sprout Social

| Plan | Annual effective / seat / month | Profile allowance | Relevant offer |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Essentials | $79 | 5 | Scheduling, optimal times, profile/post reports, Trellis AI |
| Standard | $199 | 5 | Inbox/collaboration, monitoring, reviews |
| Professional | $299 | Unlimited | Competitor/paid insights, AI post enhancement |
| Advanced | $399 | Unlimited | Reply AI, sentiment, API/helpdesk, team reports |

Essentials explicitly costs **$99/seat/month with monthly billing**. Five profiles and one user therefore cost $99 monthly or $948 annually; five users cost $495 monthly or $4,740 annually ($395/month equivalent). These are seat-count examples, **not a verified match for Cadence's proposed approval roles**. The page lists 100 monthly AI-agent credits per seat. Listening and Premium Analytics are separately priced sales add-ons for Standard and above. No permanent free plan was established. [Official pricing](https://sproutsocial.com/pricing/).

## Implications and checks

- Planable challenges paid extra seats: its Pro price does not increase with collaborator count, but does increase with workspaces and add-ons.
- Sprout already sells unlimited profiles and AI-assisted analysis; these cannot establish Cadence's uniqueness. Its larger per-seat bills leave a possible small-business price opportunity, which must also beat the value of cheaper competitors.
- A source-to-draft workflow with visible evidence and later offline AI are hypotheses to test, not proven gaps or released Cadence advantages.
- Arithmetic was independently recomputed. Account type, publishing format, provider permissions and analytics coverage still require product-level checks. No AI quality, usability or publishing reliability benchmark ran.
