# Buffer and Later: official pricing and feature evidence

Plan-scope note: this evidence was compiled before the later Free-tier revision. Any Cadence one-account/no-AI comparison below is historical; current Free scope is three networks with one account per network (three total), plus limited alt-text/analytics AI. Use the [current requirements](../Cadence-Requirements-and-Decisions.md) and [updated competitive brief](../Cadence-Competitive-Pricing-and-Positioning.md). Competitor price observations are unchanged.

Retrieved **5 October 2026**. Public standard web plans, USD, excluding applicable tax. Annual figures are a 12-month commitment, not monthly instalments. No account created or checkout completed. Buffer and Later's live annual pricing pages and current official help articles were read; Later's monthly toggle could not be operated in the available browser environment.

## Buffer

| Plan | Month-to-month | Annual commitment | Connections / users |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 3 connected social accounts; 1 user |
| Essentials, first 10 channels | $6/channel | $60/channel/year ($5/month equivalent) | Purchased channels; 1 user |
| Team, first 10 channels | $12/channel | $120/channel/year ($10/month equivalent) | Purchased channels; unlimited users |

Additional channels use marginal bands: 11–25 cost $4/month or $40/year on either paid plan; 26–50 cost $3/month or $30/year. A channel means one social account, rather than an entire network. [Official pricing and band table](https://support.buffer.com/articles/buffer-pricing-and-features-6pJrOPuzIt), [live pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing).

| Exact account scenario | Essentials: monthly / annual total | Team: monthly / annual total |
|---|---:|---:|
| 5 accounts, including Cadence's five networks | $30 / $300 | $60 / $600 |
| 10 accounts | $60 / $600 | $120 / $1,200 |
| 25 accounts | $120 / $1,200 | $180 / $1,800 |
| 50 accounts | $195 / $1,950 | $255 / $2,550 |

Calculated from the marginal bands above. **Five accounts and five users require Team**, $60/month or $600/year. Essentials covers one user only. Buffer lists LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X integrations. [Buffer pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing).

Free includes 10 queued posts per channel at a time (slots refill after publication), 100 ideas, unlimited drafts, AI Assistant and 30-day Insights. Paid plans include unlimited scheduled posts subject to a disclosed 5,000-post/channel fair-use cap, advanced analytics and unlimited history. Team adds channel permissions and approval workflows. AI Assistant is described as having unlimited credits; Free's AI reply suggestions are separately capped at five/week. [Buffer pricing](https://buffer.com/pricing).

AI performance observations and next-post prompts are **already competitive features**: Buffer's Insights provides Takeaways, repost suggestions and prompts grounded in previous performance; clicking a prompt opens its AI composer. These are available across its plans, including Free. [Insights documentation](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/using-insights-in-buffer-x4gLauQU5a), [plan availability](https://support.buffer.com/articles/buffer-pricing-and-features-6pJrOPuzIt).

Publishing and metrics still depend on account type and format. X Insights lacks channel impressions/views; Facebook Reels metrics sit outside its full Insights view; Instagram analytics requires a Professional connection. These are examples of coverage limits, not universal guarantees about every post format. [X documentation](https://support.buffer.com/articles/using-xtwitter-with-buffer-nA84XnWtuU), [Facebook documentation](https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/using-facebook-with-buffer-jY1AM9fz2G), [Instagram documentation](https://support.buffer.com/articles/using-instagram-with-buffer-YSjg2dXFV8).

## Later

| Plan | Annual effective monthly / annual total | Implied standard monthly* | Social Sets / users / posts per profile |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Starter | $18.75 / $225 | $25 | 1 set; 1 user; 30/month |
| Growth | $37.50 / $450 | $50 | 2 sets; 2 users; 180/month |
| Scale | $82.50 / $990 | $110 | 6 sets; 4 users; unlimited posts |

One set contains one profile on each supported network: Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Pinterest, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube and Snapchat. **X is absent from the current official supported list**, so there is no like-for-like all-five Cadence network price. [Social Set definition](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059362253-Choose-a-Later-Social-Plan). Growth/Scale annual add-ons: $11.25/month per set, $3.75/month per user, $3.75/month for 100 AI credits. [Live pricing](https://later.com/pricing/).

\*Monthly plan prices are **arithmetic inferences**, annual effective price divided by 0.75, using the live page's stated standard 25% annual discount; they were not verified by switching the monthly UI. The FAQ explicitly gives $5/month for additional users. Earlier help copy still says four free months; current live cards say three/25%, so use the live figures and verify checkout before buying. [Live pricing](https://later.com/pricing/), [older annual wording](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059362253-Choose-a-Later-Social-Plan).

A four-network, one-user example is Starter $225/year. A four-network, five-user example is Growth plus three users: $585/year ($48.75/month equivalent), or $65/month inferred. These cover LinkedIn/Facebook/Instagram/TikTok; they **exclude X** and leave other purchased profiles unused. Calculation, not a checkout quote.

Starter/Growth/Scale include 5/50/100 monthly AI credits; one credit gives three ideas or one caption. Later's current feature guide says caption generation supports all platforms, English only; tone/rephrasing is desktop/iOS, ideas desktop beta. Free exists in official help: 12 posts/profile/month, no AI credits and a calendar limited to two weeks past/future. Free's full connection eligibility was not verified here; do not assume eight free profiles. [Current feature guide](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/40920284785687-Reference-Guide-Plan-Availability-for-Later-Social), [free-plan explanation](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360059362253-Choose-a-Later-Social-Plan).

Growth adds approvals, roles, inbox and richer planning; Scale adds custom analytics and competitor/industry listening. Current accounts have Instagram/Facebook/TikTok/Threads histories of up to 3 months/1 year/2 years. Pinterest stays at three months; LinkedIn analytics is personal-account only. Instagram audience analytics requires 100 followers. [Analytics eligibility](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/32581160979479-Later-s-Analytics-Data-by-Plan).

Scale already offers category-based popular/niche trend summaries, AI explanations and seven-/14-day hashtag predictions. Listening is desktop-only and updates hourly. Its own documentation allows insufficient-data results: prediction is not guaranteed performance. [Future Insights](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/33212399449879-Social-Listening-Future-Insights).

Later's pricing page and newer help conflict on Starter AI add-on eligibility and some file-size limits. Treat those as unresolved for quoting. Growth/Scale user/set add-ons are confirmed; desktop subscriptions are required, and removing a connected profile or user alone does not stop an add-on charge. [Add-on management](https://help.later.com/hc/en-us/articles/360055240773-Manage-Later-Plan-Add-ons-Users-Social-Sets).

## Implications for Cadence — hypotheses to validate

- Cadence Free (one account, no AI) is less generous than Buffer on those dimensions. Its benefit must come from a valuable workflow or upgrade path; avoid calling it the most generous free option.
- AI captions, AI analytics and niche trends are already sold. A better opportunity is to combine owned source material, per-brand evidence, explainable recommendations, format adaptations and review in fewer steps. Validate time saved with users; these are planned capabilities.
- Unlimited Professional accounts could make costs predictable for multi-brand operators, but Buffer's volume discounts and unlimited Team users materially narrow the comparison. Cadence must cost hosting, storage, APIs and support before fixing price or claiming savings.
- Cadence's five included Teams logins are convenient, but Buffer already includes unlimited users. Paid extra seats must be justified by account scale and workflow value.
- Cadence's planned offline native AI is a possible distinction for private drafting, subject to real-device quality tests. These sources establish hosted product AI features; they do not prove every competitor lacks offline AI.
- Cadence's accepted manual TikTok/X handoffs reduce initial automation parity. Clearly label manual versus automatic publishing by network, account and format rather than promising universal auto-posting.
