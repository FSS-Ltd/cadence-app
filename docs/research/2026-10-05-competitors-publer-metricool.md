# Publer and Metricool competitor evidence

Plan-scope note: this evidence was compiled before the later Free-tier revision. Any Cadence one-account/no-AI comparison below is historical; current Free scope is three networks with one account per network (three total), plus limited alt-text/analytics AI. Use the [current requirements](../Cadence-Requirements-and-Decisions.md) and [updated competitive brief](../Cadence-Competitive-Pricing-and-Positioning.md). Competitor price observations are unchanged.

Research date: **5 October 2026**. Public official pages and live public pricing controls inspected; no account created or purchase attempted. Currency is vendor-displayed USD unless stated. Annual figures are the effective monthly price with a yearly commitment; taxes are excluded. This is a dated comparison, not a binding checkout quote.

## Comparison basis

Both examples require one LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X account: **five social accounts**, with one account per network. The team example has **five total users**, including the owner. Cadence permits manual handoffs where needed; competitors' paid subscriptions include API integrations, so equal account coverage does not establish equal publishing capability.

Publer figures were verified by setting the [pricing calculator](https://publer.com/plans) to five accounts and zero/four additional members, then checking both billing frequencies. The owner is included. Its UK display also showed Business at £26.46 monthly / £21.17 annual effective for one user, and £35.53 / £28.43 for five users. GBP conversions can change; do not treat them as fixed UK list prices. VAT is additional.

Metricool's live [pricing controls](https://metricool.com/pricing/) showed Starter with five brands at $25 monthly / $20 annual effective, and Advanced with fifteen brands at $67 / $53. Equivalent displayed EUR bases were €20 / €16 and €54 / €43; no GBP quote was displayed. Its [current X help page](https://help.metricool.com/x-twitter-access-in-metricool-2bt66) adds $10 or €10 per connected X account each month, or $120/€120 annually. Thus Starter is $25 + $10 = $35 monthly and $240 + $120 = $360 annually. Older official blog references to $5 are superseded by the current pricing/help controls.

Metricool counts a brand as one business/customer with **one account per network**. The five different networks fit within one brand; five accounts on the same network would require five brands. Advanced includes unlimited team members, so the five-user example needs no seat surcharge. [Brand and team access guide](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-manage-your-clients-brands-in-metricool-fkod6), [pricing FAQ and comparison](https://metricool.com/pricing/).

## Publer: offer and limits

- Free: one user/workspace, three supported accounts excluding X, ten pending scheduled posts per account, twenty-five drafts and only twenty-four hours of published history. Cadence Free's one-account allowance is narrower. [Plan definitions](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh).
- Professional: paid X support, unlimited scheduling/drafts/workspaces, bulk scheduling, media library, RSS, retained post history and team collaboration. “Unlimited” scheduling remains subject to daily/provider limits. [Professional features](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-1srlp9n/).
- Business adds unlimited AI prompts, advanced analytics/reports, best posting times, competitor and hashtag analysis, recycling/recurrence, advanced approvals and eligible API/MCP access. Accounts and extra members affect the bill; every tenth account/additional member is free. Enterprise is a custom quote. [Plan definitions](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh).
- AI already covers brand voice, captions tailored to each network, rewriting/completion, hashtags, image alt text, comment replies, images and SEO-oriented blog writing. Dedicated bio/display-name audit products were **not verified**; a general writing assistant could still draft those. [AI feature page](https://publer.com/features/ai-assist).
- Trending-post discovery already exists, including category/platform filters and a free limited tool. Paid full access is advertised; Professional also includes trending industry-news recommendations. A niche trend page alone is not unique. [Trending-post explorer](https://publer.com/tools/marketing-trending-posts), [Professional features](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-1srlp9n/).
- Paid X is included within its paid account pricing; no separate X surcharge appeared in the selected quote. A reminder/manual solution already handles some API-limited formats. [Supported-post FAQ](https://publer.com/plans).

## Metricool: offer and limits

- Free: one brand, twenty posts per month, thirty days of analytics, five competitor profiles, AI allowance; LinkedIn and X excluded. Starter adds LinkedIn, larger brand brackets, reports, retained analytics and paid integrations; Advanced adds roles, approvals, unlimited collaborators, API and customizable reporting. Custom is quoted. [Plan definitions](https://help.metricool.com/plans-add-ons-and-api-access-explained-xux1u).
- AI text allowances: Free **five credits per month**, Starter **twenty per brand/month**, Advanced **thirty-five per brand/month**. Credits depend on tokens, not a guaranteed number of captions. They reset, do not roll over, and additional credits cannot currently be bought. [AI credit rules](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-create-posts-with-metricools-ai-text-generator-sv0j8).
- AI alt text already exists for supported networks, including carousel images individually. Advanced/Custom include unlimited use; Starter/Free consume credits. Text can be reviewed, changed and translated. [Alt-text guide](https://help.metricool.com/how-to-add-alt-text-to-your-images-using-ai-mkl42).
- Metricool Studio already creates AI reports, charts, written insights, best-time analyses and comparisons from connected-account data. It cannot ingest external CRM/Google Analytics/image/video inputs; it is web-only. Current help permits two views and ten versions/view without the add-on, while pricing says five versions: **the public sources disagree**, so do not claim an exact version allowance. [Studio help](https://help.metricool.com/what-is-metricool-studio-o4pac).
- Full Studio/Advanced Analytics access is an account-wide add-on, starting at $12/month on Starter and $36/month on Advanced. Brand count affects the quote. The five-network one-user example is therefore **at least $47 monthly** with this add-on; the team example **at least $113**. These are starting-price arithmetic, not a configured checkout quote. [Add-on rules](https://help.metricool.com/plans-add-ons-and-api-access-explained-xux1u).
- Competitor tracking, hashtag monitoring, niche ideas and copy variants already exist; these are not proof of a comprehensive live trend feed on every network. Dedicated bio/display-name audit tools were not verified. [Official overview](https://metricool.com/what-is-metricool/).
- Instagram/TikTok features constrained by APIs can require notification publishing. Cadence's manual handoffs are necessary operational coverage, not by themselves a market differentiator. [Publishing tutorial](https://metricool.com/metricool-mega-tutorial/).

## Implications for Cadence — analysis, not vendor claims

1. **Captions, alt text, generic trends and AI analytics are parity features.** Cadence should connect approved original project material, account-specific results and niche signals to a concrete next-post recommendation, showing evidence, data freshness, uncertainty and an editable multi-format draft. Validate time saved and repeat posting before claiming superiority.
2. **Creator must offer a clear five-network bundle**, with AI costs and fair-use allowances explicit when funded. Publer Business already supplies five accounts plus AI/analytics for $35 monthly; Metricool Starter covers more than one brand but charges extra for X. “AI included” alone cannot justify a premium.
3. **Unlimited social accounts can matter to small agencies** facing per-account/brand pricing, but it creates real media, job and provider costs. Keep unlimited connection count distinct from unlimited storage, AI compute and publishing volume; sustainable fair-use limits remain needed.
4. **Five included Teams users are commercially sensible but not the most generous comparator.** Metricool Advanced includes unlimited collaborators. Additional Cadence seat charges need value in permissions, approvals, client workspaces or an attractive base price; no extra-seat price is decided by this research.
5. **Offline native AI is a possible later differentiator**, subject to measured device performance and eligibility. The reviewed pricing/help pages describe hosted AI workflows; this limited review does not prove that no competitor offers offline models.
6. **Cadence Free needs a strong single-account experience**, because Publer Free connects three accounts and Metricool Free covers several networks in one brand. A richer history, useful library and clear workflow may offset fewer connections; test conversion instead of assuming the smaller free allowance is an advantage.

## Evidence limits and verification

- Official pages, public calculators and help articles were read on the date above. No provider connections, post publishing, account creation, tax/checkout validation or product trials occurred.
- Calculator states were observed directly; USD/EUR pricing and UK Publer display were not converted using an invented exchange rate. Arithmetic above was checked; annual commitment is separated from monthly billing.
- Network/format support remains account- and permission-dependent. Model versions, AI output quality, response speed and competitor promises were not independently benchmarked.
