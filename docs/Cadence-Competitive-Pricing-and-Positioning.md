# Cadence: competitive pricing and positioning

Research date: **5 October 2026**. Decision: how to position and price Cadence after the FSS pilot, without building a generic scheduler with an AI button. Evidence: eight competitors' official public pricing, product pages, help articles and selected live calculators. Recommendations below are hypotheses; no customer interviews, product trials or willingness-to-pay tests have been run.

## Recommendation

Start with **owner-run service businesses and small teams that have useful work to share but little time to turn it into regular content**. This is a proposed initial audience, based on Cadence starting inside FSS, rather than a confirmed market-size finding.

The value to prove is: **turn your real work into a consistent publishing routine, then use the results to decide what to share next**. Scheduling reliability and a simple review workflow must earn trust first. Later, AI should connect approved business facts, project material and available account results to editable suggestions with visible evidence. Basic AI writing, a trend page and unlimited account connections are already sold elsewhere; they do not establish uniqueness.

Keep the agreed plan structure. Price against Publer, Buffer, Metricool and Planable first; comparisons only with expensive enterprise suites would exaggerate the opportunity. Treat offline native AI as a later possibility, pending device and quality tests.

## Cadence's potential advantage

| Opportunity | Why it could matter | Proof required before making a claim |
|---|---|---|
| **Real business material becomes useful posts** | A business could reuse approved project facts, photos, lessons and service information across accounts with fewer repeated inputs | Compare the same task in Cadence and two competitors; measure preparation time, factual corrections and drafts the user accepts |
| **An understandable next-post decision** | Connect an account's actual results and permitted niche sources to a suggested topic, reason and editable draft | Show source/date and data gaps; measure useful recommendations and repeat use. Buffer and others already offer performance-driven prompts, so the proposed end-to-end experience must be better |
| **Evidence-aware video coaching** | Explain measured attention changes, align an exact published cut/transcript where possible and suggest a concrete editing experiment | Prove correct metric definitions, source intervals and transcript mapping; separate fact, possible explanation and test. Coaching alone already exists in analytics tools; finer workflow detail must save users time without overstating causation or available data |
| **Predictable costs as accounts grow** | Flat Professional/Teams connection pricing may suit multi-brand operators facing per-channel or per-workspace charges | Cost realistic 5/10/25/50-account workloads and publish operational allowances. Buffer has volume discounts; Hootsuite and Sprout already offer unlimited profiles |
| **Simple five-person collaboration** | Owners and partners could share account access, approval responsibilities and status without building a complex process | Demonstrate safe roles, clear review and fewer missed/duplicate posts. Unlimited users elsewhere mean five included seats alone adds no unique value |
| **Private drafting without internet, later** | Certified native devices could use approved downloaded material for text help offline | Benchmark actual devices, memory, output quality, licence, battery and model-download size. This review does not prove that every competitor lacks offline AI |

For the initial public web release, the usable product advantages to earn are reliability, low-friction preparation/review and account-growth value. AI analytics, fresh trends and offline generation remain future capabilities. Offline drafting cannot publish posts or fetch fresh niche trends without a connection.

## Weaknesses to address openly

1. **Free is closer to competitors on connection count, with selective AI.** Cadence now allows three accounts across three selected networks, closer to Buffer's three channels and Publer's three supported accounts excluding X. Buffer also provides free AI writing/performance takeaways; Cadence's funded Free AI is limited to alt text and analytics, with quotas undecided. This is a competitive entry point to test, not evidence of the most generous or market-leading free offer. [Buffer](https://buffer.com/pricing), [Publer](https://publer.com/help/en/article/what-are-publers-plans-and-pricing-15h4yqh).
2. **Some initial posting is manual.** Approved handoffs cover TikTok/X and unsupported formats, but reduce automation parity with established paid tools. Show automatic/manual capability before scheduling; measure handoff completion. Other schedulers also require reminders for some native formats, so neither universal automation nor uniqueness is established.
3. **AI is deferred.** Until funded, do not advertise the planned AI catalogue as available or charge for its promised future delivery. Creator's current value is multi-network management; public pricing should match what is actually released.
4. **Fresh data is a constraint.** Hosting a free model does not provide free social listening or unrestricted analytics. Use permitted sources, visible freshness and clear coverage; AI cannot invent missing metrics.
5. **More connections create costs.** Unlimited account count requires measured storage, media delivery, job and support costs. A five-seat bundle with paid extras must also stand up against unlimited-collaborator alternatives.

## Pricing recommendation: test prices, not commitments

The current offer is three-network/three-account Free with selective funded AI, a five-network Creator bundle with broader AI, flat-connection Professional, and Teams with five seats. Competitor evidence provides comparison points; it does **not** establish customer willingness to pay or a sustainable margin. Finite Free AI allowances need real cost/quality measurements before activation.

After a successful pilot, test the following simple **monthly GBP hypotheses**, excluding VAT where applicable, with no annual discount promised yet:

| Plan | Initial price to test | Value that must exist at purchase |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | £0 | Three-network/three-account planning and reliable supported routes; limited alt-text/analytics AI only when funded/released within agreed allowance |
| Creator | £15/month | Five-account multi-network workflow; broader eligible AI only when actually released |
| Professional | £29/month | Unlimited connections with published media/throughput allowances; Creator capabilities |
| Teams | £49/month | Professional capabilities, five logins, account permissions and review workflow |
| Additional Teams seat | £5/month | One additional active/reserved member capacity; roles do not grant extra social API rights |

These are **recommended test anchors, not user-approved prices**. Publer's observed five-user Business quote is about £35.53/month before VAT, so £49 Teams would need better value for account growth or demonstrably useful workflows; it would not be the cheapest five-account option. No lower-cost or margin claim is supported without Cadence usage data.

Before setting prices: measure the pilot's hosting, storage, transfer, publishing and support load; add funded AI cost per accepted result; model heavier accounts and abuse; test purchase intent with the actual released product. Define included media, publishing and AI usage independently from unlimited account connections. Decide whether client-separated workspaces are separately priced before targeting agencies; current research does not grant unlimited workspaces.

## Effect on the phased build plan

The [full engineering plan](plans/2026-10-05-cadence.md) retains its architecture and delivery order. This research changes emphasis:

1. **Foundation:** implement the confirmed account/seat entitlements and counters, plus reliable publishing, permissions and approvals. Prepare brand facts and source provenance as already planned AI inputs; do not add a paid listening dependency now.
2. **FSS pilot:** measure weekly preparation time, completed publications, missed/manual handoffs, corrections and support problems. Keep per-network results separate; no causal engagement-lift claim from a short pilot.
3. **Commercial validation:** test the same representative tasks in Publer and Buffer/Planable using authorised accounts only when the user chooses to run trials. Suggested gate: repeat use and a meaningful measured reduction in preparation effort, with no serious posting/access defects. Choose exact targets before the comparison; they are not observed results today.
4. **Funded AI:** release bounded Free-and-above alt-text/analytics features separately from broader Creator+ source repurposing, captions and niche discovery. Prepare video/time-bin/transcript provenance contracts early; deterministic reporting from authorised manual/API evidence comes before model interpretation. The diagnostic explains measured facts, possible causes and a next editing test; replacement copy is a separately gated paid task. Keep coaching/generic generation as existing competitive functionality unless a measured workflow advantage is demonstrated.
5. **Native/offline:** test against the real iPhones and certified device profiles before selling offline AI. Do not promise every 8GB device will work well solely because of RAM.

Suggested positioning direction, separate from the recovered official brand tagline: **Cadence helps small teams turn real work into consistent content, with a clear plan for what to post next.** Validate this message; do not claim market superiority or guaranteed growth.
